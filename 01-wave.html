Lesson 1: What is a wave?

A wave is something that goes up and down, again and again, in a regular pattern. Only two numbers describe this simple wave.

Amplitude is how far the wave goes up (and down) from the middle line. In sound, bigger amplitude means louder.

Frequency is how many full up-and-down cycles happen in one second, measured in hertz (Hz). In sound, higher frequency means a higher pitch.

Period is the time one full cycle takes. It is just 1 ÷ frequency: faster waves have shorter cycles.

Try this
  1. Move only the amplitude slider. The wave gets taller or shorter, but the number of cycles stays the same.
  2. Move only the frequency slider. The height stays the same, but more cycles fit into the 1 second shown.
  3. Count the cycles on screen and check that the count equals the frequency.
